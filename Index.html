SIMPLE AND TRANSPARENT

How AgriConnect Works

One platform connecting flower farmers, seed suppliers, buyers and florists.

01

Farmers List Flowers

Farmers share flower varieties, quantities, prices and availability dates to reach potential buyers.

02

Buyers Find Matches

Buyers search and compare flowers based on their requirements, quantity, location and budget.

03

Orders Get Managed

Farmers and buyers manage flower requirements, review order details and coordinate deliveries.

04

Farmers Request Seeds

Farmers explore available flower seeds, compare varieties and prices, and submit seed requests.

05

Seed Suppliers Manage Stock

Seed suppliers list seed varieties, update prices and stock quantities, and manage incoming requests.

06

Florists Discover Flowers

Florists discover flower varieties and connect with suppliers to source flowers for their business.

OUR PURPOSE

Helping Every Flower Find Its Market.

AgriConnect brings floriculture farmers, seed suppliers, buyers and florists together through flower availability, seed sourcing, requirement-based matching and data-driven market insights.
